# Attribution and provenance

Lucide Extensions is an independent collection of filled and duotone adaptations. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lucide Icons.

The icon names, categories, tags, aliases, and underlying designs originate from [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide), snapshot `d672fc43dbf60962d9d829e35bc3b3e5ed89a178`. The catalog includes all 1,890 SVG names in that snapshot, including three upstream-deprecated names. Multi-category membership is intentional: 3,767 placements per style across 42 categories.

The adaptations were reviewed in [Paper](https://app.paper.design/file/01M4X7ABDPS9FKKG3FTC271A46). Approved geometry is stored in `icons/`, with SHA-256 checksums in `metadata/icons.json`. Default duotone secondary opacity is 22%.
